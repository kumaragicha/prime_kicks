# Prime Kicks — Animated Explainer Script

**Purpose:** convince existing offline resellers/retailers to start ordering on the Prime Kicks website.
**Format:** 2D animated character explainer, 10 scenes × 8 seconds = **80 seconds**.
**Tone:** professional, confident, problem→solution. No jokes, no hard sell.

---

## Before you generate

**Brand colours (keep identical in every scene)**

| Use | Colour |
| --- | --- |
| Text / outlines | `#171716` (near-black) |
| Background | `#FFFFFF` white, or `#F5ECDF` warm cream |
| Highlight / CTA | `#B99255` (gold) |

**Typography on screen:** bold uppercase for headlines, sentence case for small text. Max 6 words per screen.

**Character sheet — paste into every scene prompt so the characters stay consistent**

> **RAVI** — Indian man, 30s, shoe-shop owner. Short black hair, light stubble, navy half-sleeve shirt, dark trousers. Friendly, slightly stressed at the start, confident later. Flat 2D vector style, clean thick outlines, warm cream background.
>
> **CUSTOMER** — Indian woman, late 20s, casual kurta and jeans, holding a phone. Curious, then impatient, then happy.
>
> **Style:** flat 2D vector animation, minimal shading, smooth motion, no realistic faces, no text artefacts in the artwork.

**Music:** light corporate/optimistic instrumental. Scenes 1–4 subdued, lift at Scene 5, full energy from Scene 7.

**Fill in before recording:** `[WEBSITE-URL]` → your live site address.

## The script

### Scene 1 · 0:00–0:08 — The question you can't answer

**Voiceover:** "Your customer wants one specific pair. Size nine, in black. You want to say yes — but you can't. Not yet."

**On screen:** `SIZE 9. BLACK. IN STOCK?`

**Visual prompt:** Inside a small sneaker shop. CUSTOMER points at a sneaker on the wall and holds up her phone showing a photo. RAVI stands behind the counter, smiling but hesitant, one hand raised in a "one minute" gesture. Camera slowly pushes in on Ravi's uncertain face.

---

### Scene 2 · 0:08–0:16 — The wait begins

**Voiceover:** "So you message your supplier, and you wait. Ten minutes. An hour. Your customer is still standing in front of you."

**On screen:** `WAITING FOR A REPLY…`

**Visual prompt:** RAVI turns away and types on his phone. A chat bubble floats up with three animated typing dots that never resolve. Behind him, a wall clock spins fast. CUSTOMER checks her watch, shifts her weight, looks toward the door.

---

### Scene 3 · 0:16–0:24 — Every question, another call

**Voiceover:** "Then she asks for another colour. Another model. Another size. Every question means another call, another wait."

**On screen:** `ANOTHER COLOUR? ANOTHER CALL.`

**Visual prompt:** Split screen. Left: CUSTOMER asking, with three thought bubbles appearing — a red sneaker, a white sneaker, the number 8. Right: RAVI on a phone call, papers on the counter, question marks rising above his head. Both halves stack up clutter as the scene progresses.

---

### Scene 4 · 0:24–0:32 — The order you lost

**Voiceover:** "Meanwhile the shop next door already showed her a photo and a price. That order is gone. And it happens every week."

**On screen:** `LOST ORDER`

**Visual prompt:** CUSTOMER walks out the door, waving politely. Through the window, she is seen entering a competitor's shop where a shopkeeper holds up a phone with a bright product photo. RAVI alone at the counter, phone still in hand. Muted, desaturated colours.

---

### Scene 5 · 0:32–0:40 — Prime Kicks, online

**Voiceover:** "This is Prime Kicks online. Every product, every size, every colour — live, on your own screen."

**On screen:** `PRIME KICKS · ONLINE`

**Visual prompt:** Bright transition — cream background sweeps in, colour returns. RAVI opens a laptop; a clean product grid of sneakers animates into place, tile by tile. Gold accent glow around the screen. His expression shifts from tired to interested.

---

### Scene 6 · 0:40–0:48 — Real stock, in real time

**Voiceover:** "Check real stock instantly. Filter by brand, model or size. If the website shows it in stock, it is in stock."

**On screen:** `LIVE STOCK · EVERY SIZE`

**Visual prompt:** Close-up of the laptop screen as a UI animation: size chips (7, 8, 9, 10) get tapped, the grid filters down, and green "in stock" badges pop onto three products. A gold tick animates beside a size-9 row. Ravi's finger taps the trackpad.

---

### Scene 7 · 0:48–0:56 — Send photos and videos instantly

**Voiceover:** "Download product photos and videos in one tap, and send them to your customer while she is still standing there."

**On screen:** `PHOTOS + VIDEOS · ONE TAP`

**Visual prompt:** A download icon pulses on the laptop; three product images and a small video thumbnail fly out of the screen and land inside a phone chat window held by RAVI. On the phone, CUSTOMER'S reply bubble appears with a thumbs-up. Energetic, quick motion.

---

### Scene 8 · 0:56–1:04 — Order it yourself

**Voiceover:** "Place the order yourself, at your reseller price. No phone calls, no waiting for our team to reply."

**On screen:** `ORDER IN SECONDS`

**Visual prompt:** RAVI clicks an "Add to cart" then a gold "Buy now" button; a progress bar fills and a large gold tick stamps onto the screen with a confetti burst of three small sneaker icons. A crossed-out telephone icon fades away beside him.

---

### Scene 9 · 1:04–1:12 — Your full order history

**Voiceover:** "And every past order stays in one place — what you bought, what you paid, and where your shipment is right now."

**On screen:** `ORDER HISTORY · TRACKING`

**Visual prompt:** The laptop shows a clean list of past orders; rows slide in one by one with dates and amounts. One row expands into a three-step delivery tracker (packed → shipped → delivered) with the second step glowing gold. RAVI nods, arms folded, satisfied.

---

### Scene 10 · 1:12–1:20 — Call to action

**Voiceover:** "Answer in seconds. Sell more, every single day. Log in at [WEBSITE-URL] — Prime Kicks. Stock you can see."

**On screen:** `[WEBSITE-URL]` · `PRIME KICKS`

**Visual prompt:** Wide shot of a busy, bright shop: RAVI hands a shoe box to a happy CUSTOMER while two more customers browse. Pull back and dissolve into a clean end card — white background, Prime Kicks logo centred, website URL below in gold, small sneaker icon animating in.
