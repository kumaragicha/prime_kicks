# Prime Kicks — Scene Generation Prompts

Ten self-contained prompts, one per 8-second clip, matching
[prime-kicks-video-script.md](./prime-kicks-video-script.md).

## How to use these

1. **Generate each clip silent.** Add the voiceover and on-screen text as overlays in Google Vids afterwards — AI video models render lettering badly (misspelt words, warped logos), which is why every prompt ends with a "no text" instruction.
2. **Never edit the STYLE or CHARACTERS lines.** They are byte-identical across all ten prompts on purpose: that repetition is what keeps Ravi looking like the same man in scene 9 as in scene 1.
3. **Generate 2–3 takes per scene** and keep the best. Expect to re-roll scenes 6 and 7 more than the others — screen-inside-a-screen shots are the hardest for the model to keep clean.
4. **Aspect ratio:** prompts say 16:9. For Instagram or WhatsApp status, change to `9:16 vertical` and re-generate — don't crop the 16:9 clip, the characters lose their heads.
5. **Order of work:** generate scenes 1, 5 and 10 first. If those three look right, the style is locked and the rest will match.

## Scene 1 — The question you can't answer

```
8-second 2D animated clip, 16:9.
STYLE: flat 2D vector animation, clean thick black outlines, minimal flat shading, warm cream #F5ECDF background, near-black #171716 linework, gold #B99255 accents, smooth confident motion, simple geometric shapes, no gradients.
CHARACTERS: RAVI — Indian man, early 30s, short black hair, light stubble, navy half-sleeve shirt, dark trousers, friendly face. MEERA — Indian woman, late 20s, mustard kurta with blue jeans, long dark hair in a low ponytail, holding a smartphone.
SETTING: interior of a small sneaker shop, wall shelves of shoe boxes and three sneakers on display, wooden counter in the foreground.
ACTION: Meera stands at the counter pointing up at a sneaker on the wall with one hand and holding her phone screen toward Ravi with the other. Ravi behind the counter smiles politely and raises one open palm in a "give me a minute" gesture, eyebrows slightly raised, uncertain.
CAMERA: slow push-in from a medium two-shot toward Ravi's face, ending on a medium close-up.
MOOD: warm but tense, a question hanging in the air.
NEGATIVE: no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no brand names, no photorealism, no facial close-up distortion, no camera shake.
```
